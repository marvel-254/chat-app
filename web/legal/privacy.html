Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • NYX Version 25

At NYX, your privacy is our foundational design principle. We believe personal messaging should feel calm, private, and free from surveillance or behavioral profiling.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Basic credentials required for authentication (email and username) securely managed via Supabase Auth.
  • Direct Messages: Messages sent between participants, isolated through PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies so only conversation participants can query them.
  • Presence & Delivery Signals: Ephemeral realtime broadcasts (such as active typing state and read receipt timestamps) that enable synchronized conversation flow.

2. Information We Never Collect

  • No third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics pixels.
  • No address book, contacts scanning, or harvesting.
  • No GPS background location tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of user data with third parties.

3. Security Architecture

Conversations in NYX are isolated using database-level Row-Level Security (RLS). Database queries enforce that users can only select, insert, or receive realtime changes for chats in which they are an authenticated participant.

4. Your Rights and Data Control

You retain complete ownership over your data. You may request account deletion or export your conversation records at any time. Because NYX is open-source under the MIT license, you can inspect the client and server schema directly on GitHub.
