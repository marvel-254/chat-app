Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • BRO Version 25

BRO is committed to minimal data collection. We only utilize strictly necessary, privacy-preserving session tokens and cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text fragments stored on your device that facilitate authenticated sessions, security verification, and basic preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Cookies We Use

  • Session Authentication: Cryptographically verified tokens that keep you securely signed in to BRO between app launches.
  • Security Tokens: CSRF protection tokens and rate-limiting keys used to prevent denial of service.

3. What We Never Use

  • No Marketing Cookies: We do not set advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: We do not track your activity across third-party websites or services.
  • No Analytics Pixels: We do not include third-party data broker beacons.
